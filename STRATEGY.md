# Neryn content strategy (draft, 2026-11-13)

**Goal:** booked demo calls from mid-level business owners (multi-outlet, salaried staff). Views are only the first step.
**Funnel:** short video → one keyword comment (CALL, …) → DM with 2 questions → 15-minute demo → setup.

## 1. What to measure (per video, after 48 h)
| Stage | Metric | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Stop the scroll | YouTube "viewed vs swiped away"; Instagram 3-second hold | YT Studio / IG Insights |
| Hold | Average watch time, rewatches | both |
| Spread | **Sends per reach** (shares to DMs) | IG Insights |
| Intent | Keyword comments, DMs | comments |
| Money | Demos booked, setups sold | your sheet |

Instagram's head (Mosseri, Jan 2025) named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top Reels signals. YouTube tests each Short on a small audience and widens it if people keep watching. Exact thresholds quoted online come from vendors; compare against our own numbers instead.

## 2. Three content pillars, 4–5 posts a week
| Pillar | Length | Per week | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Agent episodes** (Comment → Build) | 50–60 s | 2 | Story: mystery open → problem → "போதும்." → named agent → reveal → one CTA. Leads. |
| **B. Money math** | 15–25 s | 2 | One number that hurts: "4 outlets, 2 staff, இருந்தும் 40 missed calls." Cut from episode assets. Reach + retention. |
| **C. Can you tell? / live demo** | 20–40 s | 1 | Screen-record of the agent on a real call, objection answers ("Tamil-ல சரியா பேசுமா?"). Trust + sends. |

Every video ends with one ask. Pillar B/C ask: "உங்க friend-ஓட கடைக்கு இது தேவைப்படும்னா, send பண்ணுங்க." (drives sends).

## 3. How to pick a problem (score 1–5 each, take the highest)
1. Mid-level: branches, team, salaries already spent on it
2. ₹ loss you can say in one line
3. Happens every day/week (not seasonal)
4. A named agent can do it live on screen (call, WhatsApp, review reply)
5. Different agent skill from the last 2 episodes
6. Tamil Nadu flavour (gold schemes, admission season, festival rush)

## 5. Format rules (every episode)
- Proof in the first 2 seconds (agent already working), question on screen from frame 1.
- Named agent, never the word "AI" in script; brand line "Neryn agent".
- One surprise or self-aware beat (reveal, "நானும் ஒரு agent தான்").
- Owner's line: staff go back to customers (no "replace your staff").
- ≤ 60 s, captions on every line, one keyword CTA, no music.
- Post 7–9 PM IST on Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts (+ Facebook).

## 6. Leads: what happens after "CALL"
1. Reply within 2–3 hours (manual for the first videos, then a Meta-approved comment-to-DM tool).
2. DM asks: how many outlets? how many calls/enquiries a day?
3. Offer a 15-minute demo. **Have one live demo agent ready before the first lead** (Mithra on a test number).

## 7. Learn and adjust
- After 10 videos: keep the pillar with the best sends/reach and the best keyword-comment rate; cut the weakest.
- Instagram Trial Reels (testing a Reel on non-followers) unlock at 1,000 followers; use them for pillar B hooks.
